ENTRY FORM

3. Product Print Name (Urdu)  ← used only for printing
4. Category (Dropdown)

Replace the existing "Volume" field.

Example values:

- Shalwar
- Kameez
- Dupatta
- Embroidery

5. Scale (Dropdown)

Replace the existing Scale implementation.

Display Urdu values:

- سیٹ
- سوٹ
- تھان
- نگ

Internally the database may continue storing English enum values if required, but the operator must only see Urdu labels.

10. Remarks

------------------------------------------------

BUSINESS LOGIC

Do NOT calculate anything inside Product Entry.

Instead store these values for later billing.

The ERP billing engine will use them.

Formula:

Total Length

=

Scale Quantity × Length

Example

8 Sets

×

30 Gaz

=

240 Gaz

Bill Amount

=

240 × Rate

Example

240 × 250

=

60000

Do NOT show Total Amount in Product Entry.

That belongs to Purchase Bill / Sales Bill.
